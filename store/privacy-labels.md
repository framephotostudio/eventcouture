# App privacy answers

## Apple: App Privacy ("nutrition label")
- **Data used to track you:** None. No advertising, no ad IDs, no tracking prompt needed.
- **Data linked to you:** None. There are no accounts.
- **Data not linked to you** (recommended answers for version 1.0):
  - User Content > Photos or Videos. Purpose: App Functionality. Not linked to identity, not used for tracking. (Inspiration photos are sent to our server and Google's AI to create concepts.)
  - User Content > Other User Content. Purpose: App Functionality. Not linked, not tracking. (The event description, city and guest count are sent to create concepts and find venues.)
  - Contact Info > Name. Purpose: App Functionality. Not linked, not tracking. (When a host shares an invitation, guests' RSVP names, plus-ones and dietary needs are stored on our server so the host can see them; deleted 30 days after the event.)
    - User Content > Other User Content (quote requests). Purpose: App Functionality. Not linked, not tracking. (When a host taps Get quotes, the city, kind of vendor, date, guest count, theme, budget for that item and any note are stored on our server for listed vendors to answer. No name or contact details are included. Requests are deleted 14 days after the event date.)
  - Contact Info > Email Address. Purpose: App Functionality (and Developer's Advertising or Marketing only if you send newsletters). Not linked, not tracking. (Only when someone signs up for planning tips; they can unsubscribe from any email.)
  - Contact Info > Name (reviews). Purpose: App Functionality. Not linked, not tracking. (A review keeps the first name the reviewer types; reviews are approved by hand before they show.)
  - User Content > Photos or Videos (moving or sharing a plan). Purpose: App Functionality. Not linked, not tracking. (Continue on another device uploads a copy of the plan, including its photos, for 30 days; a view-only plan link stores the plan and its photos until the host stops sharing.)
  - User Content > Other User Content (reviews and plan copies). Purpose: App Functionality. Not linked, not tracking. (Review text; the plan copy for Continue on another device; vendor replies to quote requests.)
- Usage Data and Diagnostics: not collected. The app itself runs no analytics (Google Analytics only runs on the website).
- Plans, guest lists and photos stay on the phone. Only a shared invitation (event name, date, place, dress code, polls, gift list) and the RSVPs and votes guests send back are stored on our server, and they're deleted 30 days after the event or when the host deletes it.
- If Google's AI ever keeps requests (free Gemini plan), the answers above still apply; just make sure the Privacy Policy says so.

## Google Play: Data safety
- Data collected: no analytics in the app. Photos and the event description are processed to create concepts (ephemeral). Personal info > Email address (planning tips sign-up, optional). Personal info > Name (guest RSVP names, review first names). Photos (plan copies for moving or sharing, optional). Other user content (reviews, vendor replies, quote requests).
- Data shared: None.
- Encrypted in transit: Yes.
- Users can request deletion: Yes (Delete event removes the plan from the phone and its shared invitation and RSVPs from our server).
- Data collected: Personal info > Name (guests' RSVP names, for app functionality, not shared).
