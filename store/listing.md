## Description
Plan a beautiful event from one photo, a few words, or just your colors.

Event Couture by Always Luxe turns inspiration into a complete plan:
- Three event concepts made for your occasion, colors and budget
- A detailed budget with every cost, sized to your city and guest count, including local tax and service charges
- Cultural weddings planned with their own traditions, music and food: Arabic, Indian, Pakistani, Persian, Turkish, Mexican, Chinese, Vietnamese, Korean, Filipino, Nigerian, Moroccan, Greek, Spanish, Russian, African American and multicultural fusion, each with its own playlist
- Venues and vendors near you, matched to your occasion and theme: build up to 3 booking packages and compare them side by side, swap anything, remove what you don't need
- Get quotes from vendors without sharing your name or number, or message vendors you found on Instagram in one tap
- A guided booking checklist: one vendor at a time, with the message written for you
- Formal invitations written for your event, with names, monogram, dress code and reply date; guests RSVP, vote and see your gift list from any phone
- Menu ideas, a playlist, what to buy to get the look, a checklist with due dates and a day-of schedule
- Guest list, seating, cost splitting and thank-you notes
- Your finished plan in one place: save it as a PDF, email it or share it
- Works in over 60 countries and 250 cities, from the US and Canada to the UK, Europe, the Middle East, India, Asia, Australia and Latin America, with prices in your currency
- Reserve a table at restaurants in one tap

Not sure where to start? Browse inspiration by occasion: birthdays, kids' themed birthdays, showers, weddings, quinceañeras, Sweet 16s, graduations, holiday parties, corporate events and more. Inspiration photos are AI-generated samples, clearly labeled.

Free, with no sign-in. Put your date in your calendar and get reminders before the big day.

## App Review notes (paste into App Store Connect > App Review Information > Notes)
Event Couture plans a whole celebration from a description or inspiration photos: three looks, a budget sized to the city with local tax and service charges, three side-by-side packages of top-rated venues and vendors, cultural wedding traditions (Arabic, Indian, Pakistani, Persian, Mexican and more), invitations with guest RSVPs, a seating chart, checklist and day-of timeline.

No sign-in is needed and there is nothing to buy.

To try it:
1. Tap Plan. Type "black and gold 30th birthday for 40 guests in Houston" and tap Continue. Choose the type of event, the city, the guest count and where it is (for example Event venue or banquet hall), then tap Create my looks.
2. The first time, the app asks before anything is sent to Google's Gemini AI. Choose Allow (or Not now to use the built-in style library). Choose one of the three looks.
3. The app opens Budget & bookings and builds three packages of top-rated venues and vendors from Google Maps, side by side. Tap Swap to change any line, or Choose to start the booking checklist.
4. Tap Message on any vendor line to send them a request (no name or contact details). The vendor answers from a private link, and the reply shows on that line with Report and Block.
5. On the plan's Overview (My events, then the plan), tap Add to Calendar (the system event sheet) and Remind me (local notifications 30, 14, 7 and 1 day before the event; an event date must be set).
6. Your finished plan: Save as PDF and Share (the system share sheet). Guests & invitations, then Invitation and event page, then Send invitation shares the guest link.

Native features: tab bar, system share sheet for PDFs and backup files, Add to Calendar, local reminder notifications, haptics, an offline notice with plans still available offline, an in-app browser for links, and the Android back button.

User content: new vendor listings are approved by us before they show; rude words are filtered in vendor replies, listings, RSVPs and reviews; every vendor reply has Report and Block; reports reach us within the hour and are handled within 24 hours. Reviews of the app are approved by hand.

Privacy: plans stay on the device. Only shared invitations, guests' RSVPs, vendor requests and replies, and reviews are stored on our server. No analytics, ads or tracking in the app. Inspiration photos are AI-generated and labeled. Founder’s Pick businesses are chosen by the founder and are not paid placement.
